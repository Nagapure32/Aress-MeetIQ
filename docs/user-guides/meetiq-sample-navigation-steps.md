# MeetIQ Sample Navigation Steps

Use the following as a sample step-by-step navigation script for the MeetIQ platform.

1. Navigate to the MeetIQ web application URL.
2. Click on the **Email** or **Username** field.
3. Enter your MeetIQ user email or username.
4. Click on the **Password** field.
5. Enter your password.
6. Click on the **Login** button.
7. Verify that the **Dashboard** page is displayed.
8. Click on the **Search meetings, tasks...** field.
9. Enter value **Meetings** in the search field.
10. Click on **Meetings** from the search results.
11. Verify that the **Meetings** page is displayed.
12. Click on the meeting filter button.
13. Select **All** from the meeting filter menu.
14. Click on the first meeting row in the meeting list.
15. Verify that the meeting detail page is displayed.
16. Click on the **Transcript** tab.
17. Review the available transcript lines.
18. Click on the **AI Chat** tab.
19. Click on the prepare or reindex chat button if chat is not ready.
20. Click on the chat message field.
21. Enter value **Summarize this meeting** in the chat message field.
22. Click on the send button.
23. Verify that an AI response is displayed.
24. Click on **Back to meetings**.
25. Click on **Tasks** from the left navigation menu.
26. Verify that the **Task board** page is displayed.
27. Click on the task search field.
28. Enter value **follow-up** in the task search field.
29. Select a task row from the task list.
30. Click on the **Status** dropdown in the task details panel.
31. Select **In progress**.
32. Verify that the task status is updated.
33. Click on **Dashboard** from the left navigation menu.
34. Verify that the Dashboard page is displayed again.

## Upload Recording Sample

1. Navigate to the MeetIQ web application URL.
2. Log in with valid user credentials.
3. Verify that the **Dashboard** page is displayed.
4. Click on the **Upload recording** button.
5. Click on the **Meeting title** field.
6. Enter value **Client sync recording** in the Meeting title field.
7. Click on the **Meeting date** field.
8. Select or enter the meeting date and time.
9. Click on the **Recording file** field.
10. Select a valid audio or video recording file.
11. Click on the **Upload** button.
12. Verify that the recording upload request is submitted.
13. If processing completes immediately, verify that the meeting detail page is displayed.
14. If processing continues, navigate to **Meetings** and verify that the uploaded recording appears in the meeting list.

## Meeting Assistant Settings Sample

1. Navigate to the MeetIQ web application URL.
2. Log in with valid user credentials.
3. Verify that the **Dashboard** page is displayed.
4. Click on **Settings** from the left navigation menu.
5. Verify that the **Meeting assistant settings** page is displayed.
6. Click on the **Enable auto-join** toggle.
7. Click on the **Require approval** toggle.
8. Click on the **Approval lead** field.
9. Enter value **2** in the Approval lead field.
10. Click on the **Look ahead** field.
11. Enter value **15** in the Look ahead field.
12. Click on the **Save settings** button.
13. Verify that the page displays **Settings saved** or **No unsaved changes**.
